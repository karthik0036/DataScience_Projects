## 🔹 1. The Price Is Right – End-to-End Agentic AI Deal Hunting Platform

**Source Folders:** Project_6.1_CAPSTONE,Project_6.2_CAPSTONE Project_6.3_CAPSTONE,

### 📌 Overview
Built an end-to-end AI platform that predicts product prices and autonomously identifies undervalued online deals using Amazon product data, machine learning, fine-tuned LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and multi-agent AI systems. The platform processes over 94K product records, leverages fine-tuned Llama 3.2 models for domain-specific pricing, queries an 800K+ product vector corpus via ChromaDB, and orchestrates decentralized AI agents to automatically find and notify users of active deals. Deployed using Modal serverless infrastructure with an interactive Gradio interface.

### 🛠 Concepts Covered
- **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT):** LoRA, QLoRA, TRL, and 4-bit Quantization
- **Frontier & Open-Source LLMs:** Llama 3.2 (3B), OpenAI, and high-speed inference via Groq
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Dense Embeddings, Sentence Transformers, and ChromaDB Vector Search
- **Agentic AI Architecture:** Multi-agent orchestration (Scanner, Specialist, Planner, and Messenger Agents)
- **MLOps & Cloud Deployment:** Serverless infrastructure via Modal, interactive UI building with Gradio, and experiment tracking with Weights & Biases
- **Data & Predictive Engineering:** Synthetic dataset structuring, NLP pipelines, and PyTorch Neural Networks
